# Play, appearance, and efficient iteration

Use this alongside the playable-toy workflow when a game depends on substantial art or a team is spending many passes refining it.

A functional blockout proves the interaction and integration. A convincing still proves one view of the art. Neither establishes the other. Keep separate evidence for the requested play loop, visual foundation, asset behavior, performance, and required human judgments.

## Put the highest uncertainty first

Identify what could force the largest rebuild: the primary interaction, a focal asset's silhouette, a moving assembly, its performance, or its readability through the game camera. Make a small representative test of that uncertainty before expanding features and detail.

For a hero asset, establish major shapes and shared interfaces in actual neutral and gameplay views before dense accessories and final LODs. Use the available modeling skill for construction and `randroid-clean-slop` for visual specificity. Do not invoke art workflows to fix an input bug or redesign a working genre to satisfy a screenshot checklist.

Keep requested depth in scope. An inspection game may need hidden hinges and engine parts; a distant racing camera may not. Prioritize by the user's actual viewing and interaction modes. Detail that depends on an unfinished roof, body, or cabin should wait for those structures to stabilize.

## Use evidence that can disprove the claim

Pixel motion, elapsed frames, a valid mesh, and passed unit tests each prove limited things. A spinning object is not evidence of a satisfying driving loop. Exercise the input, intended consequence, resistance, failure/recovery, and relevant feedback; inspect the resulting motion. Keep subjective enjoyment as a human judgment when required.

Probe failures can be measurement failures: the wrong camera, an unready asynchronous state, missing imported assets, or a capture outside the play area. Reproduce with a small case and verify the observation before modifying working gameplay. Await the actual completion signal within a bounded deadline rather than repeatedly extending arbitrary sleeps. Preserve production contracts while repairing the instrument.

## Recover from stalled refinement

Keep one short current-state record with the accepted build, active candidate, highest-impact open defect, next experiment, and invalidated evidence. Use it after context changes instead of reconstructing the plan from dozens of trial folders.

When the same major defect survives comparable attempts, reassess its representation or design relationship. Do not fill the interval with optional microdetail or more scaffolding. When specialists are already authorized, use stable inputs and disjoint ownership, then integrate a coherent candidate before final capture and broad verification.

Pilot each distinct capture path before long recordings. Run focused checks during experiments and required full gates at the proper delivery checkpoint. Preserve valid evidence and repeat affected checks when inputs change; never waive a required check to shorten iteration.

Stop refinement when the requested acceptance is met and deliver the verified artifact. If only a required human judgment remains, provide the concrete build and review evidence. Continued autonomous work should address an identified machine-actionable gap, not an unbounded instruction to make everything better.
