# Specificity in games and 3D assets

Review what the player actually sees and does. Distinctive work comes from coherent choices tied to the brief; a new palette, extra noise, or more meshes does not establish identity.

## Establish what would make this asset belong here

Identify the requested visual style, setting, focal asset, player camera, and inspection distance. Pick a few observable relationships from appropriate references: silhouette, proportion, construction, materials, wear from use, or spatial composition. Separate engineering facts from styling choices. Keep reference identity and licensing boundaries intact.

Use a specificity check: if names, logos, and UI labels disappeared, which visible or interactive choices would still express this game's setting and purpose? If the answer is only color or added decoration, improve the forms, spatial relationships, or behavior that carry that purpose. Familiar genre conventions and intentional minimalism can be right for the brief; do not add novelty merely to escape a checklist.

## Inspect causes in priority order

| Observed tell | First investigation | Evidence of improvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A focal object still reads as assembled boxes or plates | Primary silhouette, cross sections, surface continuity, intentional style | Whole-object neutral views and gameplay-distance view |
| Detail-rich wheels, fasteners, or upholstery surround an unfinished body | Dependency order and allocation of effort | Major form improves before further optional detail |
| Bright strips, seams, dents, or lobed highlights dominate joins | Actual geometry, contact, triangulation and normals | Same camera/light, joint crop plus whole object |
| A black finish reads oddly bright, flat, or plastic | Lighting/exposure, geometry, paint response and material separation | Neutral and relevant game lighting; no hidden geometry defect |
| Random dirt, bevels, emissive trim, or texture noise everywhere | Whether each treatment follows use, fabrication, or the chosen style | Simplified comparison preserves useful detail and clearer hierarchy |
| Repeated props or terrain make every place interchangeable | Distribution rules, scale, landmarks, navigation and local context | Views along actual player movement, including transitions |
| Polished labels describe behavior absent from the world | The real interaction and feedback | Input produces the intended observable consequence |

Use ordinary light to expose form. Keep an appealing hero view, but also look at the sides and the difficult junctions it hides. Concept images can guide construction; they cannot serve as screenshots of completed work. A technically valid mesh or a green gameplay probe is evidence for its stated contract only.

## Prevent refinement from becoming a loop

Keep each finding concrete: artifact/view, visible problem, likely cause, severity at the intended distance, proposed change, and confirming comparison. Prioritize major form, functional readability, and structural joins before decoration. Finish requested close-inspection detail after its surrounding surfaces stabilize.

After a change, look at the whole object or scene again. A fixed crop may coexist with a worse silhouette or a newly exposed seam. If comparable attempts keep moving the defect to a neighboring surface, reconsider the shared construction or design relationship rather than accumulating patches. Continue the task with that revised approach.

Keep a compact accepted baseline and a current candidate. State what changed perceptually and which prior evidence became stale. Do not measure progress by screenshots produced, polygon counts, report length, or subjective scores without a comparison.

For a game, sample the actual play camera and affected motion as well as the asset viewer. For a UI, return to its responsive and interaction checks. Final human art, usability, and enjoyment decisions stay with the user when required. These rules introduce no additional approval gate and do not authorize a genre change.
